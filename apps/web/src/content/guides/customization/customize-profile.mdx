## Introduction

Personalize your AI model by setting a custom name and profile image.  
This guide walks you through the steps to make your AI interactions more unique and engaging.

## How to Customize Your AI Model Profile

### **Go to the Models Section**

Log in and navigate to the _Models_ section from the sidebar.

### **Edit Profile**

Click the profile image area to upload and crop a new image; the change will apply immediately without needing to click _Save_.  
 To change the name, use the input field and click _Save_ to apply the update.  
 You can also click _Remove Custom Image_ to revert to the default image.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What image formats are supported?**  
A: You can upload PNG or JPEG images, with a size limit of 1MB.

**Q: How long can the model name be?**  
A: Model names can be up to 20 characters long.

**Q: Can I revert to the default profile?**  
A: Yes, click _Remove Custom Image_ in the edit profile section to restore the default image.
